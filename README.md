# NJAMA GROUP

Parent organisation and group operating platform for a portfolio of independent, specialist businesses and applications.

## Governing principles
- Business independence with coordinated group intelligence.
- NJAMA INTELLIGENCE operates within each business and across the group, using only authorised data and actions.
- The owner retains ultimate authority; no business action is taken without explicit authority.
- Subsidiaries retain their own specialist identities, operations and data boundaries.
- Multi-currency operations, with KES as the initial reporting currency.
- Minimalist, elegant and timeless group identity.

The detailed architecture and implementation plan will be maintained under `docs/`.
